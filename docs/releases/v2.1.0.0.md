# MCSkin3D v2.1.0.0

## What's new

- The first time you open a player skin, the editor checks its arm layout and selects Alex or Steve. A five-second notice appears if the model changes. Any model you choose afterward is remembered, including after a restart.
- Importing by Minecraft nickname uses the model from the player's Mojang profile and marks the skin as checked immediately.
- Hover over a face, edge or corner of the preview cube to highlight it. Click to snap the camera to that view, or drag from the cube to rotate freely.
- The chest icon on the toolbar opens two character-shaped sets of visibility controls for the main and outer layers. Both fit within the existing tool-options height.
- Folder arrows in the skin explorer stay clickable after resizing or scrolling. The explorer opens directly to the skin tree, with the duplicate Parts tab removed.
- The startup splash appears before editor initialization. Its artwork and credits scale together, and it now credits karilaa-dev as maintainer.

## Download or update

From v2.0.0.0, open **Help > Check for updates** and choose **Update and restart**.

For a new Windows x64 installation, download **Minimal**, extract it into a writable folder and run **MCSkin3D.exe**. Choose **Full** if you also want the sample skins, palettes and overlays. Both packages include the .NET runtime. SHA-256 hashes for both ZIPs are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
